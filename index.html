Piece Finder

Personal prototype

Drop in an outfit.
Find the pieces.

Add any photo of a look. The AI picks out each piece and sends you to matching searches across South African stores.

Pictures come from Google Shopping in South Africa, so they cover whichever stores Google lists. Links open each store's own search in a new tab. Stores marked "via Google" use a site-restricted Google search because their own search address is not reliable to link to.

Settings

Pick which stores show up. Saved in this browser.
